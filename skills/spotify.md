Spotify was not installed on the test Seeker: every label here is from Spotify's help pages (unverified).

Screens
Bottom tabs: Home, Search, Your Library, and Premium or Create.
Home: recent plays, made-for-you mixes, and the profile picture at the top left for Settings.
Search: the search field, and genre and mood tiles to browse.
Your Library: playlists, albums, artists and podcasts, with filters across the top and the plus to create a playlist.
Mini player: the song playing, just above the tabs. Tap it to open the full player.
Full player: the add button (plus or check) to save to Liked Songs, shuffle, previous, play or pause, next, repeat, the devices icon for speakers and TVs, share, and the queue.

Common tasks
Playing something: Search, type a song, artist or playlist, then tap it.
Liking a song: in the player tap the plus; it goes to Liked Songs in Your Library.
Making a playlist: Your Library, the plus, then Playlist, and name it.
Adding to a playlist: the three dots next to a song, then Add to playlist.
Downloading: open a playlist or album and tap the download arrow. Premium only.
Playing on a speaker: in the player tap the devices icon and pick the speaker.
Audio quality: profile picture, Settings and privacy, then Audio quality.

Warnings
On the free plan the phone app plays with ads, some playlists shuffle only, and skips are limited.
Premium renews every month until cancelled in the account page on the website.
