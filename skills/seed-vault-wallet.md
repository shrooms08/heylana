Screens
Home: the Balance at the top, then Swap, Receive, Send and Earn (Kamino). Buy is in the top right corner. Tokens are listed below with their values.
Receive: shows the wallet address and QR code, and the Seeker ID (.skr name) if there is one.
Amount screen: a number pad, Max (the whole balance of that token) and Continue.
The Send sheet: Sending (amount and token), To, From, Network fee, then the Send button.
Addresses are shortened on screen, like 7c2y...SxSv. Compare the first and last four characters with who you meant.

Common tasks
Paying someone:
1. Tap Send on Home.
2. Pick the token.
3. Enter the recipient's address or .skr name.
4. Type the amount, or Max, then Continue.
5. Read Sending, To and Network fee, then tap Send.
6. Confirm with the fingerprint (double-tap the side button, then touch the sensor).
Getting paid: tap Receive and share the address, QR code or .skr name.
Trading tokens: tap Swap, pick both tokens and the amount, check the rate (unverified).

Warnings
A transfer cannot be undone once confirmed. Check To against the first and last four characters.
Max sends the whole balance of that token.
The first time someone receives a token, the network charges about 0.002 SOL to open their token account.
No real app or person ever asks for the seed phrase.
Surprise tokens or NFTs with a claim link are a common scam.
