As of September 2026 there are two independent validator clients. Agave is maintained by Anza, forked from the original Solana Labs validator whose repository was archived in January 2025; the current line is Agave 3.x. Firedancer is written from scratch by Jump Crypto: the hybrid Frankendancer — Firedancer's networking on Agave's runtime and consensus — has been on mainnet since 2024, and the full Firedancer client went live on mainnet in December 2025. Roughly 14% of stake runs full Firedancer and about 26% Frankendancer, the rest Agave. Documentation pages describing Firedancer as testnet-only are stale.
