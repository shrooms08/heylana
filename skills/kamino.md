Walked on this Seeker 2026-09-19 up to Deposit; nothing deposited.

What it is
Home has an Earn section: "Earn 4.06% on your USDC", "Powered by kamino", Start. USDC put in goes to Kamino's lending market, where borrowers pay interest. The rate moves with demand; it is not promised. The deposit stays the user's and can be withdrawn anytime.

Screens
Start with no USDC: a sheet offering "Receive crypto".
Start with USDC: two intro pages.
1. "Earn on your USDC with Kamino", how the lending works, Next. The footer: Kamino's Terms of Use apply; Kamino is not affiliated with Solana Mobile.
2. The rate in large type, "Earn more on the USDC in your wallet", three points (yield compounds with nothing to claim; no lockup, though withdrawals may wait in rare busy periods; audited 20+ times, no bad debt to date), an (i) icon, Continue.
Deposit: a dollar amount with a keypad, "Available" (the USDC balance), "Earn APY", a note that Continue accepts the USDC Earn Vault Disclosures and Terms, and Continue, greyed until there is an amount.
After a deposit: the balance and withdraw screen (unverified: not walked).

Common tasks
Putting USDC in: under Earn tap Start, Next, Continue, type the amount, Continue, then check the amount and network fee before confirming.
Taking USDC out: from the Earn section once there is a deposit (unverified).

Warnings
Not a bank account and not insured; lending carries smart contract risk.
Only USDC goes in. Keep a little SOL for fees.
Nobody from Kamino or Solana Mobile will message asking to move funds to a "new vault".
