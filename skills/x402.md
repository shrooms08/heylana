What it is
x402 is an open standard for paying on the web. A site, an API or an AI agent answers a request with HTTP 402 Payment Required and lists what it wants: an amount, a token, a network and who gets paid. The wallet pays, usually in USDC, and the request is tried again with proof of payment.
On a phone it shows up as a pay-to-continue prompt in a browser, a dApp or an agent, followed by the wallet's signing screen.

What to check before paying
Amount: the exact figure, and whether it is per request or repeats.
Token: usually USDC. A token called USDC that is not Circle's mint is not USDC.
Network: Solana mainnet, or devnet for play money. The wallet and the request have to agree.
Recipient: who is paid. Compare the address ends with the address the site itself publishes.
What is being bought, and whether it is a one-off.

Scam patterns
A 402 prompt on a site that has no reason to charge.
An amount far above the price on the page, or extra zeros.
A token allowance or delegation instead of a single payment.
A recipient that changes between tries, or does not match the site.
Countdowns and "pay now or lose access" pressure.
A page asking for the seed phrase. No payment ever needs it.
Payments on chain are final. When in doubt, leave the page and check the site first.
