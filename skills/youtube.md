Checked on this Seeker: the bottom tabs and the search bar. Watch page labels are unverified.

Screens
Bottom tabs: Home (the feed, with topic chips across the top), Shorts, Create (the plus), Subscriptions, You.
Top bar: Cast, Notifications (the bell) and Search (the magnifier).
Search: the query field, Clear, and Voice search (the microphone). Results can be narrowed with the filter icon.
You: History, Playlists, Watch later, Liked videos, Downloads and Your videos.
Watch page: Like and Dislike, Share, Download, Save, then the channel with Subscribe, the description and Comments. Tapping the video shows pause, the settings gear and full screen.

Common tasks
Searching: tap the magnifier, type or tap Voice search, then pick a result.
Saving for later: on the watch page tap Save, then Watch later or a playlist.
Captions: tap the video, then the gear, then Captions.
Playback speed or quality: tap the video, the gear, then Playback speed or Quality.
Subscribing: tap Subscribe under the video; the bell next to it sets notifications.
Clearing history: You, then History, then the three dots, then clear.

Warnings
Downloads and background play need YouTube Premium.
Comments and descriptions often carry links to fake giveaways and "double your crypto" offers. No real giveaway asks you to pay first.
Live streams using a famous name to promote a token or wallet address are a common scam.
