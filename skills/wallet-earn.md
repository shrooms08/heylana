Home, read on this Seeker 2026-09-21: Balance; "Swap", "Receive", "Send"; an "Earn" section; "Tokens" with "View all". The Earn row: "Earn 4.09% on your USDC", "Powered by kamino", and "Start". The screens after Start were walked on 2026-09-19 up to Deposit; nothing deposited.

What it is
USDC put in goes to Kamino's lending market; borrowers pay the interest. The rate is an estimate that moves: 4.06% on 2026-09-19, 4.09% on 2026-09-21. Say it as "about", with the day it was read. The deposit stays the user's.

Earning on USDC
1. On Home, under Earn, tap "Start".
2. With no USDC: a sheet offering "Receive crypto".
3. With USDC: "Earn on your USDC with Kamino", tap "Next"; the rate page, tap "Continue".
4. Deposit: type the amount. "Available" is the USDC balance, "Earn APY" the rate. "Continue" stays greyed until there is an amount and accepts the vault's Disclosures and Terms.
5. A review with the amount and network fee follows (unverified).
Heylana stops at that review screen and tells the user what they are about to sign. It never taps confirm and never approves; the signing glance takes over.
Withdrawing: from Earn once there is a deposit (unverified).

Confusions
APY is a yearly rate if it held; it compounds, nothing to claim. No lockup, though withdrawals may wait when busy. No minimum was shown. Only USDC goes in; keep some SOL for fees.

Warnings
Not a bank account, not insured. Nobody from Kamino or Solana Mobile asks you to move funds to a "new vault".
