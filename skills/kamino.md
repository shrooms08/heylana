What it is
Earn on the Wallet's Home screen is a USDC Earn Vault. USDC put in is lent out through Kamino's lending market, and it earns interest from borrowers.
The rate (APY) moves with demand to borrow. It is not a fixed or promised return.
The deposit stays the user's; the wallet shows the vault balance growing over time (unverified).

Screens
Earn opens the vault: the current rate, the amount deposited, and buttons to deposit and to withdraw (unverified: exact labels).
Every deposit and withdrawal is a transaction confirmed with the fingerprint, with a small network fee in SOL.

Common tasks
Putting USDC in:
1. Tap Earn on Home.
2. Choose to deposit and type the USDC amount (unverified: button name).
3. Read the amount and fee, then confirm with the fingerprint.
Taking USDC out:
1. Tap Earn on Home.
2. Choose to withdraw and type the amount (unverified: button name).
3. Confirm with the fingerprint. Withdrawals are normally quick, but can wait if the market is fully borrowed (unverified).

Warnings
Not a bank account and not insured. Lending protocols carry smart contract risk and can lose value.
Only USDC goes into this vault. Keep a little SOL for network fees.
Nobody from Kamino or Solana Mobile will message asking to move funds to a "new vault".
